# Fab Lab Fort Smith

Fort Smith's community makerspace. 3D printers, laser cutters, vinyl cutters, an electronics lab, and a growing community of makers who show you how to use them. Open to everyone.

We're a community-owned makerspace in downtown Fort Smith, and a launchpad for whatever you've been meaning to make. We put the tools in your hands. You bring the vision and the nerve to make it your own. Do cool stuff with what you've already got, fix what bugs you, and build up the world around you. No prior experience needed. Everyone starts somewhere.
